About Me

When I’m not working on a project or honing my skills, you’ll find me exploring new ways to integrate technology with everyday life. Whether it’s through personal projects, data analysis, creative pursuits, or finding better ways to solve everyday problems, I’m always looking for opportunities to learn, grow, and make a meaningful impact.
